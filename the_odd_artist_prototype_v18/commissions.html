How it works

The Commission Process

01

Tell me the idea

Send the type of work, reference material, approximate size, deadline, and what you want the piece to feel like.

02

Scope & quote

We clarify the project, materials, timeline, and price before work begins.

03

Create the work

I develop the piece and communicate at the points where feedback is useful for the project.

04

Finish & deliver

The finished work is prepared for pickup, shipping, installation, or the final delivery method we agreed on.
